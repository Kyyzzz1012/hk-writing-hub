HK Exam Success Formula

How HK Primary Writing Exams Are Graded

Breakdown of the 4 key criteria markers look for in Primary 4 and Primary 5 assessment papers.

1

Content (30%)

Relevant to prompt, detailed explanation of ideas, clear purpose and logical flow without off-topic sentences.

2

Language & Tenses (30%)

Correct subject-verb agreement, accurate tenses (Past tense for stories/recounts, Present tense for letters/opinions).

3

Structure & Format (20%)

Proper 4-paragraph layout, standard formal/informal letter headers (Dear..., Yours sincerely), and transitional connectives.

4

Vocabulary & Tone (20%)

Upgraded vocabulary (e.g. *exhausting* instead of *tired*, *promptly* instead of *fast*), varied sentence starters, and correct spelling.

Must-Know Connectives for Primary 4 & 5

Ordering Ideas
  • • First of all, / To begin with,
  • • Secondly, / In addition,
  • • Furthermore, / Moreover,
  • • Finally, / Lastly,
Contrast & Causes
  • • However, / Nevertheless,
  • • Although / Even though...
  • • As a result, / Therefore,
  • • Because of this,
Story Transitions
  • • All of a sudden, / Suddenly,
  • • Without hesitation,
  • • To my surprise,
  • • Looking back, I realized that...
